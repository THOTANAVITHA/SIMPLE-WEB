Welcome to Malla Reddy Engineering college and Management sciences

Malla Reddy Engineering College & Management Sciences is an institution and is affiliated to JNTU, Hyderabad. The college was started in the year 2008 under the auspices of Vemula Raghava Reddy Lakshmamma Edcuational Society, The college provides a quiet and ideal setting amidst natural surroundings. It is located on Hyderabad – Nagpur National Highway (Medchal Road), Telangana, within a verdant 10 acres of serene environment which is lush green and free of pollution.
